# ShotZ 0.1.0

The first public release of ShotZ.

- Capture an area, a window or the full screen with your shortcuts.
- The quick-access stack keeps every capture at hand: copy, save, drag out or annotate.
- Annotate with arrows, shapes, text, counters, highlights and redaction, then copy or save.
- Present captures on a background with padding, gradients and shadow.
